Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026
Your notes never leave your device

The short version

Daybook is a personal notes app. Everything you write is stored only on your own device. There is no account to create, no sign-in, and no server that Daybook sends your notes to. We do not collect, see, sell, or share your data.

Information we collect

None. Daybook does not collect any personal information.

  • The notes, titles, dates, and colors you create are saved in your device's local storage, on the device itself.
  • Daybook has no user accounts and asks for no name, email, or phone number.
  • There are no servers that receive or store your content.

How your notes are stored

Your notes are kept in the app's private on-device storage. They stay on your device and are not uploaded, synced, or backed up to us. If your device's own system backup (for example, an operating-system or cloud backup you have enabled) includes app data, that backup is governed by that service's privacy policy, not by Daybook.

Analytics, tracking & ads

Daybook contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that profile you. We do not use cookies or advertising identifiers, and we do not track your activity across apps or websites.

Permissions

Daybook requests only the permissions it needs to function as a notes app. It does not access your contacts, location, microphone, or camera to collect data about you.

Deleting your data

You are in full control. Delete any note from inside the app at any time. To remove everything, uninstalling Daybook deletes all notes stored on your device. Because your notes live only on your device, we have no copy to delete on our side.

Children's privacy

Daybook does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since no data is collected or transmitted, the app does not gather information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Reach us at support@daybook.com.
